The idea

Trading is secretly math — so we teach both.

A trucker practicing the market in a rest stop is doing probability, percentages, and risk in their head. Their kid doing fractions at the kitchen table is building the exact same muscles. Our school connects the two: the free simulator makes money math tangible, and live tutoring turns it into real mastery — for the whole family.
