Why Discipline

The problem isn't your entries. It's what you do after a loss.

Most accounts don't die from bad picks. They die from the behavior that follows a red streak.

Tilt is the account killer

The revenge trade after a loss — bigger size, worse entry, no plan. One tilt session can erase weeks.

Oversizing turns a dip into a blow-up

A normal drawdown only ends your account if your size is too big to survive it. Sizing is a skill, not a guess.

Signals don't fix behavior

You can buy someone else's picks forever and still blow up. The leak is in what you do — and behavior only changes with reps.

You can't practice discipline live

With real money on the line, you can't safely rehearse the hard moments. So rehearse them here — then take the habit to your real account.

What It Trains

Reps that build the habits most traders skip

Real mechanics inside the simulator — each one drills a specific way accounts blow up.

Anti-tilt cooldown

A forced pause after a loss so you can't fire off a revenge trade. You practice waiting instead of chasing.

-3R circuit breaker

Down three risk-units on the day, the session stops you. You learn what a real daily stop feels like — and to respect it.

Size cap after a loss

After a red trade, your next size is capped automatically — so a bad run can't snowball into a blow-up.

Shakeout & re-entry drills

Practice holding through noise and re-entering with a plan — instead of getting flushed out at the worst spot.

Kelly sizing curve

See how bet size vs. edge actually behaves — why "bet big to win big" quietly ruins accounts, and how to size sanely.

Journal + leak chart

Every trade is logged, and a leak chart shows where you actually bleed — the mistakes you repeat without noticing.

3-minute centering

A short reset routine before you trade — get out of reaction mode and into your process. Uses a neutral built-in visual; no personal photos required.

FAQ

Straight answers

Is this signals or predictions?

No. We never tell you what to buy or sell. It's a practice tool for building discipline — sizing, patience, and not tilting. What you trade in your real account is entirely up to you.

Will this make me money or guarantee profits?

No — and anyone who promises that is lying to you. Trading involves real risk, including losing money. This trains skills and habits; it does not predict markets or guarantee any result.

Do I need experience to use it?

No. It's built for the habits most people skip when they start — sane sizing, waiting for your spot, and stopping when you're supposed to. Beginners and experienced traders both drill the same fundamentals.

Is it real money?

No. Everything is simulated — that's the whole point. You get to rehearse the hard moments (losing streaks, the urge to revenge-trade) with zero risk, then take the habit to your real account.

What do I actually practice?

Position sizing, patience, and anti-tilt discipline — through real mechanics: the anti-tilt cooldown, the -3R circuit breaker, size caps after a loss, shakeout & re-entry drills, the Kelly sizing curve, a trade journal with a leak chart, and a 3-minute centering reset.
