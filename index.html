FAQ

Questions drivers ask us

Do I need my own MC authority?

Most of the drivers we dispatch run under their own MC/DOT authority. If you're not sure where you stand, give us a call and we'll walk you through it.

What's your fee?

Founding drivers get a 5% intro rate (first 10 drivers); the standard rate is 10% after that. Either way — no hidden costs, no long contracts, cancel anytime.

How fast can you get me a load?

Once you're signed up and your packet is ready, we start searching right away. Timing depends on your lane, equipment, and the market — call us and we'll give you a straight answer for your situation.

Do you speak Hindi?

Yes. You can talk to us in Hindi (and Punjabi), and we negotiate with brokers in fluent English. That's the core of what we do — call us.

What areas do you cover?

We dispatch across the US. Tell us your lanes and equipment when you call, and we'll let you know exactly how we can help.

How do I get paid?

The broker pays you (the carrier) for the load. We handle the rate confirmation and paperwork and follow up so payment doesn't get stuck. Ask us about factoring options when you call.

What is TruckYeah Traders (the community)?

It's our free community for drivers who want to learn about the markets and build financial knowledge together. It's education and camaraderie only — not investment advice, and we never sell signals or promise profits. Ask us how to join.

Are you a new company?

Yes — and honestly, that's good news if you get in early. As a founding driver you get intro pricing and our full personal attention. Fewer drivers means faster answers and a dispatcher who actually knows you and your truck.
