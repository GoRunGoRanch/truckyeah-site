A time-honored tradition

Ancient protection, purification, and devotion

Across the Vedic and Hindu traditions, cow dung is treated as sacred and purifying, and is traditionally used in the protection of the home and family. The Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam describes how mother Yaśodā protected the infant Kṛṣṇa with cow dung, cow urine, and the holy names of Viṣṇu after the attack of Pūtanā; the Manu-smṛti and later accounts likewise record cow dung’s protective and cleansing role in daily and ceremonial life.

“The ladies used to know how to chant mantras to give protection to children with the help of cow dung and cow urine.” — Śrīla A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupāda, purport to Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam 10.6.22–23

At GoRunGo Ranch, our cows are surrounded by sacred Vedic hymns and devotional chanting 24 hours a day, seven days a week — nurturing an atmosphere of peace, purity, and auspiciousness.
